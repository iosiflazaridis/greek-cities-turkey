# Greek Cities in Present-Day Turkey

An interactive map, statistics and the full list of the 603 cities whose site lies in present-day Turkey
and that were Greek at some time between the Mycenaeans and the fall of Trebizond in 1461.

**Open the page:** https://iosiflazaridis.github.io/greek-cities-turkey/

- `index.html`: the page (self-contained; the data are embedded)
- `img/`: the 16 cards in full size; `thumb/`: their thumbnails
- `dl/`: the data as CSV, JSON and an Excel workbook (with a summary, the rules, and the places left out with reasons)

Sources: the Inventory of Archaic and Classical Poleis (Hansen & Nielsen 2004), Cohen's *Hellenistic Settlements*,
Roman provincial coins, Hierocles' *Synecdemus*, the bishops at the councils, the Barrington Atlas and the Pleiades gazetteer;
every record in the data keeps its own sources and notes. Map: Natural Earth.
